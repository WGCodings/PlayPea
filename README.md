# PlayPea

PlayPea is a website where you can play against every released version of my
chess engine [Pea](https://github.com/WGCodings/Pea) and analyse your games
with it.

**https://playpea.wgcodings.com**

## What you can do

- **Play against Pea.** Pick any version, from the very first one (v1.0,
  around 650 Elo) up to the latest. Early versions make good sparring
  partners, and the newest ones are a real challenge. You can choose your
  colour, how long Pea thinks per move and an optional starting position.
- **Analyse games.** After a game, open it in the analysis board to see
  Pea's evaluation, the best line, an evaluation graph, your accuracy, and
  which of your moves were good or were mistakes. You can also load games
  from PGN, chess.com or lichess.

Pea runs entirely in your browser, on your own device. Nothing is computed on
the server and there's nothing to install.

## Credits

PlayPea is a fork of [Chesskit](https://github.com/GuillaumeSD/Chesskit) by

Chesskit, with Stockfish replaced by Pea.
